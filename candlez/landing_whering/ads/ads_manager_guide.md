# 🛠️ Facebook Ads Manager: Setup & Targeting Guide

A step-by-step guide to setting up your ClosetMind validation campaign.

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1: Campaign Setup (The Budget)

1.  **Go to [adsmanager.facebook.com](https://adsmanager.facebook.com/)** and click the green **Create** button.
2.  **Objective**: Select **Awareness** or **Traffic**. (Traffic is usually better for early validation).
3.  **Campaign Level**:
    - **Name**: `Validation_ClosetMind_V1`
    - **Advantage Campaign Budget**: OFF for now. (Better to control spend per ad set).

---

## 🎯 Phase 2: Ad Set (The Targeting)

*This is where you define WHO sees your ads.*

1.  **Conversion Location**: Website (Put your Vercel URL here).
2.  **Budget**: $5 - $20 / day (Start small to test).
3.  **Audience (THE KEY PART)**:
    - **Location**: Choose your target market (e.g., United States, United Kingdom, or local).
    - **Age**: 25 - 45 (Matches your core persona).
    - **Gender**: Women (Based on the brainstorm).
    - **Detailed Targeting (Interests)**:
        - Capsule wardrobe
        - Personal stylist
        - Work-life balance
        - Productivity
        - Sustainable fashion
        - Fashion enthusiast
    - **Placements**: Select **Advantage+ Placements** (Let Facebook decide).

---

## 🎨 Phase 3: The Ad (The Creative)

1.  **Identity**: Select your Facebook Page and Instagram Account.
2.  **Format**: Single Image or Video.
3.  **Media**: 
    - Upload `ads/assets/problem_stress.png` or `ads/assets/solution_result.png`.
4.  **Copy**:
    - Copy-paste the Primary Text and Headlines from `ads/ad_copy.md`.
5.  **Website URL**: Your landing page link.
6.  **CTA Button**: Choose **"Learn More"** or **"Sign Up"**.

---

## 🧪 Phase 4: A/B Testing Strategy

**Don't just run one ad.** Run 2–3 different Ad Sets or "Creative Variations":

- **Ad Set 1 (Broad Fashion)**: Targeting anyone interested in broad fashion.
- **Ad Set 2 (Productivity/Minimalism)**: Targeting those interested in work-life balance and capsule wardrobes.
- **Ad Set 3 (Problem-focused Text)**: Using the stressful image and "Stop wasting 30 mins" text.

---

## 📊 Phase 5: What to look for (The Validation Metrics)

After 3–5 days:

1.  **CTR (Click-Through Rate)**: 
    - **< 1%**: Your hook is weak. Adjust the headline or image.
    - **> 1.5%**: Good interest!
    - **> 2%**: Strong interest.
2.  **Cost per Click (CPC)**: Ideally under $1.00 (depending on the market).
3.  **Conversion Rate (on your Tally form)**:
    - This is the ONLY validation that matters. 
    - If people click but don't sign up, your landing page isn't closing the deal. 

**Pro Tip**: Run the "Problem-focused" creative first. It usually converts best for early validation apps! 🚀
