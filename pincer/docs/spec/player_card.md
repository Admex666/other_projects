# Turn Cards: A Quick Rule Reference While Playing

## Annexing Colonies
To annex an enemy colony you must occupy the colonies' tile. During this occupation, you may collect the resources rolled for the colony but you may not build any land/naval units (from colony). If the colony has an existing port, you acquire the trade bonus; if not, you may build a port in the same color as the colony. All bonuses from connected roads will be lost during this time for both players. You may also build a fort turn after annexing (in your own color). Once your unit no longer occupies the colonies' tile, ownership is immediately transferred back to the original owner (on their turn), at which point any fort built will be destroyed.

---

## Player's Turn

### Phase I: Harvest
Roll resource die. All players collect one resource card per colony, if die matches adjacent tile (Unless occupied by enemy unit).

### Phase II: Mobilization
If desired; move any or all units by their respective limits to tile of choice, or make attacks on enemy units (Refer to 'Combat' and 'Land and Naval Units').

### Phase III: Development
Redeem resource cards to build new infrastructure, or place new units on the board. *New units may not be used until next turn!*

---

## Infrastructure

* **COLONY:** must be built at least 3 tiles apart on a tile occupied by a friendly unit. Allows player to collect resources from adjacent tiles and buy land units. *\*May not be built on edge tiles of the board.*
* **PORT:** must be built on a water tile adjacent to an existing colony. Allows player to purchase frigates and decreases exchange rate by 1, unless occupied by an enemy naval unit. Port/colony.
* **FORT:** must be built around a colony. Protects colonies by: forcing enemies to have either a cannon or frigate, plus additional land unit adjacent to the colony for a whole turn before attacking, at which point fort is destroyed. Also allows player to purchase cannons.
* **ROADS & BRIDGES:** build along the edges of tiles to connect colonies. Each new colony connected reduces exchange rate by 1 & units may transfer between connected colonies in 1 move/turn. Must be built upon an existing colony or road.
* **BRIDGES:** may be built on a water tile, edge across *\*must touch land on either side*. Allows land units to cross between connected bridge tiles. Also allows player to purchase cavalry.

---

## Land and Naval Units
*\*LAND UNITS MAY NOT ENTER MOUNTAIN TILES*

* **MUSKETEER:** move/attack by tile per turn. New units must be placed in an empty, friendly colony.
* **CAVALRY:** move/attack only by 2 tiles away per turn. May NOT move through enemy occupied tiles or mountain tiles to attack. New units must be placed in a colony connected to another by roads.
* **CANNON:** move/attack by tile per turn, may return immediately into original tile after attacking (before opponent's counter move). MUST return to its original tile after attacking naval units from land or sea. New cannons must be placed in a colony with a fort.
* **FRIGATE:** move/attack only by 1 or 2 tiles per turn. May transport one of any land unit across water tiles, all land units must be immediately adjacent to the frigate to embark but may move/attack by their respective ranges upon disembarking. All units being transported are lost upon naval defeat. New frigates must be placed in an empty, friendly, unoccupied port.

---

## Combat
Players may move or attack all of their desired units on their turn. To attack, simply move your unit into your opponent's tile and remove their piece from the board. After attacking your opponent will be able to make one immediate counter move/attack with any unit before your turn resumes. Opponent must use a separate piece for each counter attack before reusing the same one, until the offensive player's turn ends.

---

## Maneuvers
Maneuvers can be found on resource cards, shuffled into the deck. If drawn, they may be played while attacking or defending (depending on the card), by a unit, from a tile that corresponds with the maneuver's resource.

* **AMBUSH:** play before attacking! Prevent your opponent from playing a counter card to your attack.
* **OUTFLANK:** play before moving or attacking! Take one extra move/attack with the same unit.
* **RETREAT:** play once attacked! Move your attacked unit into an empty tile (by unit's range).
* **FORTIFY:** play once attacked! Deflect enemy attack, both players unit's will remain in their original tiles.
* **ENCIRCLEMENT:** play before moving or attacking! Take 2 consecutive moves or attacks (with separate units). *\*skips opponent's counter-move*

---

## Exchange Rate
* 5:1 trade (5 of the same resource in for any of your choice).
* Minus 1 for every port OR new colony connected by a road. (limit 2:1).

---

## Victory
* **DOMINATION:** Once the board is completely colonized with no room for an additional colony (as they must be 3 tiles apart), a player may declare victory if; they control 3 quarters of the colonies on the board, through building or annexing.
