# Learning: High Customer Retention & Cross-Challenge Loyalty

Data from the launch of the second challenge (Nagy-Kevély csillagai) revealed exceptionally high customer lifetime value (LTV):

## Key Insights
* **$> 80\%$ Repeat Purchase Rate:** Over 80% of buyers for Nagy-Kevély were previous finishers of the Prédikálószék challenge.
* **Near-Zero Marginal CAC for Returning Runners:** When launching a new challenge, direct email notification to existing finishers drives immediate pre-orders with zero ad spend.
* **Implication:** The primary growth engine for VitaSteps is expanding the catalog of distinct, collectible regional challenges rather than acquiring one-off buyers.
