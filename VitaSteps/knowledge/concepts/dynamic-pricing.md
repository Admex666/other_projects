# Concept: Dynamic Pricing & Cart Calculation

VitaSteps utilizes standard transparent pricing with volume options:

## Price Structure
* **Standard Entry:** **7 990 HUF** (includes 1 physical medal, digital diploma, GPX pack, Foxpost parcel locker shipping).
* **Multi-Medal Orders (Couples / Teams):**
  - 2 medals: 15 980 HUF
  - 3 medals: 23 970 HUF
  - 4 medals: 31 960 HUF
  - For each medal in a multi-medal order, individual participant names and emails are captured at checkout.
* **Home Delivery Surcharge:** **+1 200 HUF** optional upgrade for direct doorstep courier delivery instead of Foxpost locker.
* **Referral Discount:** **-1 000 HUF** coupon code applied at checkout via [[referral-program|Referral Program]].
