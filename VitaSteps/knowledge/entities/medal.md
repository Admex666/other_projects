# Medal (Fizikai Érem)

VitaSteps awards premium, heavy zinc alloy medals produced in strictly limited runs of **100 numbered pieces per challenge**.

## Manufacturing Specifications
* **Material:** Die-cast Zinc Alloy, 3D relief embossing.
* **Thickness & Size:** 5 mm thickness, 75–80 mm diameter.
* **Finish:** Antique bronze (Prédikálószék) / Antique silver with translucent enamel & luminous stars (Nagy-Kevély).
* **Engraving:** Laser-engraved sequential serial numbering (`#001/100` – `#100/100`).
* **Ribbon:** Sublimated 30 mm custom satin ribbon with quick-release loop.
* **Unit Production Cost:** ~1 630 HUF / medal (total batch investment: **163 000 HUF** for 100 pcs).

## Supplier Contacts
* Sourced from Chinese medal manufacturers via Alibaba (specifications documented in `medal/suppliers.json` and `medal/sourcing_guide_china_medals.md`).
