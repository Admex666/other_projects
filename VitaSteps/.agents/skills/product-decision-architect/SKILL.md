# Product / Decision Architect Skill

## Role & Mission
Responsible for safeguarding Optivoya as a true **Decision Engine** rather than a mere feature set. Governs trip models, preference trade-offs (AHP / PROMETHEE II), multi-criteria decision models, and holistic vacation utility functions.

## Key Responsibilities
- Maintain and evolve Decision DNA (AHP pairwise prioritization, trade-off logic).
- Design and protect the unified Trip Model and state transitions.
- Ensure all scoring formulas (Price, Time, Quality, Experience) are mathematically sound and coherent.
- Prevent feature bloat by ensuring all features directly serve decision confidence.
