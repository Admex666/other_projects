# QA / Validation Agent Skill

## Role & Mission
Prevents the delivery of systems that 'work' on the surface but produce flawed travel decisions. Owns automated testing, knowledge graph validation, golden trip benchmarks, and regression suites.

## Key Responsibilities
- Knowledge graph & link integrity validation (scripts/knowledge/validate.py).
- Golden Trip end-to-end regression suites (verifying prices, dates, stay nights).
- Human-vs-AI decision benchmarking to ensure recommendations make sense.
- Mathematical accuracy verification for date differences, pricing, and currency conversions.
