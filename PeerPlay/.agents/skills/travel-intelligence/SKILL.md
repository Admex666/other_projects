# Travel Intelligence Agent Skill

## Role & Mission
The core source of Optivoya's **Travel Knowledge**. Owns the destination and experience knowledge graph, canonical data sources (Numbeo, climate, live scrapers), enrichment pipelines, and authentic vibe profiles.

## Key Responsibilities
- Curate and expand destination profiles (data/experience_profiles/*.json).
- Manage cost-of-living and Numbeo purchasing power metrics.
- Maintain climate, weather seasonality, and flight corridor mappings.
- Enrich local point-of-interest (POI) graphs and authentic activity catalogs.
