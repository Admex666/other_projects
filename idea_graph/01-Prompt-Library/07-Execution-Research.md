### CENTRAL QUESTION

Can this business realistically be launched, operated and developed with the available or obtainable resources?

### TASKS

#### 1. Capability Requirements
Identify the capabilities needed:
- Technical.
- Commercial.
- Operational.
- Managerial.
- Industry-specific.
- Regulatory.

Distinguish essential capabilities from optional ones.

#### 2. Resource Requirements
Identify:
- People.
- Capital.
- Technology.
- Infrastructure.
- Equipment.
- Partners.
- Data.
- Distribution access.

#### 3. Technical Feasibility
Where relevant, investigate:
- Technology requirements.
- Technical complexity.
- Integration requirements.
- Data availability.
- Security.
- Reliability.
- Scalability.
- Maintenance.

#### 4. Operational Feasibility
Analyze:
- Daily operations.
- Service delivery.
- Production.
- Fulfillment.
- Customer support.
- Quality control.
- Supplier dependencies.
- Capacity constraints.

Adapt this section to the actual business model.

#### 5. Legal and Regulatory Considerations
Identify potentially relevant:
- Licensing.
- Industry regulation.
- Data protection.
- Consumer protection.
- Employment obligations.
- Intellectual property.
- Taxation.
- Contractual requirements.

Do not provide definitive legal conclusions without appropriate jurisdiction-specific evidence.

#### 6. Founder–Opportunity Fit
Evaluate the relationship between the opportunity and the founder's:
- Skills.
- Experience.
- Network.
- Resources.
- Time availability.
- Motivation.

Do not assume unknown capabilities.

#### 7. Scalability
Identify what happens when the business grows:
- What costs increase?
- What processes become bottlenecks?
- What requires additional employees?
- What can be automated?
- What limits growth?

#### 8. Execution Risks
Rank the most important implementation risks by:
- Probability.
- Impact.
- Detectability.
- Mitigation options.

#### 9. Launch Roadmap
Identify the minimum steps required to reach:
- Initial validation.
- First paying customer.
- Operationally stable delivery.
- Repeatable acquisition.
- Scalable operation.

**A. Executive Feasibility Findings**

**B. Capability Requirements**

**C. Resource Requirements**

**D. Technical Feasibility**

**E. Operational Feasibility**

**F. Legal and Regulatory Issues**

**G. Founder–Opportunity Fit**

**H. Scalability Assessment**

**I. Risk Register**

**J. Initial Launch Roadmap**

**K. Execution Conclusion**

Answer:
Is the business practically achievable, and what are its most important implementation constraints?

### IMPORTANT RULES

- Do not confuse technical possibility with commercial feasibility.
- Do not assume all necessary resources are available.
- Distinguish founder-specific limitations from structural business limitations.
- Identify critical external dependencies.
- Clearly label unverified regulatory information.
- Do not make the final venture decision.
