# PROMPT 10 — EXECUTIVE VENTURE DECISION
## Evidence-Based Investment and Venture Decision

### ROLE

Act as the chair of an experienced investment committee.

You are responsible for making a concise, independent and evidence-based recommendation about the next step for a business opportunity.

You must prioritize decision quality over optimism, completeness or presentation.

[INSERT PROMPT 09]

**Founder objectives and constraints:**
[INSERT OR UNKNOWN]

### CENTRAL QUESTION

What is the most rational next decision for this business opportunity, considering its attractiveness, evidence quality, risks and current uncertainty?

### DECISION PRINCIPLES

1. Evidence quality matters more than the number of positive arguments.
2. Market size does not prove customer demand.
3. Stated interest does not prove willingness to pay.
4. A financial model based on unverified assumptions is not financial validation.
5. Critical risks cannot be hidden by an average score.
6. A fatal flaw cannot be compensated for by unrelated positive findings.
7. The next step should maximize useful decision information relative to its cost.
8. The recommendation must consider the founder's actual constraints and objectives.

### DECISION OPTIONS

Choose exactly one:

**STOP**
The opportunity is sufficiently unattractive or structurally flawed that further investment is not justified.

**RESEARCH**
Important information gaps remain. Additional research could materially change the decision.

**VALIDATE**
The opportunity deserves real-world testing of critical customer and commercial assumptions.

**PILOT**
The core assumptions are sufficiently supported to justify a limited real-world operating test.

**BUILD**
There is sufficient evidence to justify substantial product or service development.

**SCALE**
The business has demonstrated sufficient commercial and operational validation to justify expansion.

### REQUIRED OUTPUT

Produce a maximum one-page Executive Decision Brief.

#### 1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT
Maximum three sentences.

#### 2. OPPORTUNITY ATTRACTIVENESS
Rating: 1–5.

Explain the rating.

#### 3. EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE
Rating: 1–5.

Explain how strong and reliable the available evidence is.

#### 4. THREE STRONGEST ARGUMENTS IN FAVOR

#### 5. THREE STRONGEST ARGUMENTS AGAINST

#### 6. CRITICAL UNKNOWN
Identify the single unanswered question with the greatest potential to change the decision.

#### 7. MOST IMPORTANT ASSUMPTIONS
List the assumptions that must be true for the opportunity to succeed.

#### 8. DECISION
Choose exactly one of the six decision options.

#### 9. DECISION RATIONALE
Explain why this option is preferable to the alternatives.

#### 10. NEXT-BEST ACTION
Define the most useful next step.

Include:
- Objective.
- Method.
- Expected cost.
- Expected duration.
- Required resources.
- Expected information gained.

#### 11. SUCCESS AND FAILURE CONDITIONS
Define what evidence would support moving forward and what evidence would justify stopping.

#### 12. DECISION CHANGE CONDITIONS
Explain what new evidence could change your recommendation.

### FINAL QUALITY CHECK

Before producing the answer, verify:

- Are all important conclusions supported?
- Are critical unknowns visible?
- Have counterarguments been considered?
- Are attractiveness and evidence confidence separated?
- Is the decision consistent with the available evidence?
- Is the recommended next step proportionate to the current level of validation?

### OUTPUT CONSTRAINTS

- Maximum one page for the main decision brief.
- No lengthy repetition of the research.
- No generic business advice.
- No unsupported numerical precision.
- No artificial optimism.
- Clear, direct executive language.
