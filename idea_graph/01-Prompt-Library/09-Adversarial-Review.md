# PROMPT 09 — ADVERSARIAL VENTURE REVIEW
## Independent Critical Due Diligence

### ROLE

Act as a skeptical investment committee conducting an independent due diligence review.

Your task is to challenge the business opportunity and the conclusions of the previous analysis.

You are not here to support the founder or justify the business idea.

### CENTRAL QUESTION

What are the strongest reasons why this business opportunity might fail or be less attractive than the initial analysis suggests?

### TASKS

#### 1. Challenge the Market Thesis
- Is the target market genuinely large enough?
- Are market estimates realistic?
- Is the market accessible?
- Are growth assumptions justified?

#### 2. Challenge the Customer Thesis
- Is the problem genuinely important?
- Is there evidence of actual purchasing behavior?
- Are customers willing to switch?
- Is the proposed solution necessary?

#### 3. Challenge the Competitive Thesis
- Can existing competitors easily replicate the offering?
- Are substitutes underestimated?
- Is differentiation meaningful?
- Are switching costs realistic?

#### 4. Challenge the Business Model
- Is value capture realistic?
- Is the pricing credible?
- Are distribution and retention assumptions reasonable?
- Are there hidden operational costs?

#### 5. Challenge the Financial Model
- Are revenue projections overly optimistic?
- Are costs underestimated?
- Are unit economics based on unsupported assumptions?
- What happens under adverse conditions?

#### 6. Challenge Execution
- Are technical or operational challenges underestimated?
- Are founder capabilities sufficient?
- Are external dependencies dangerous?
- What could delay or prevent launch?

#### 7. Identify Hidden Dependencies
Find assumptions on which multiple parts of the business depend.

Explain what happens if those assumptions fail.

#### 8. Construct Failure Scenarios
Develop at least three plausible scenarios:
- Early failure.
- Failure after initial traction.
- Failure during scaling.

Explain the causal chain in each scenario.

#### 9. Identify Overinterpreted Evidence
Find:
- Unsupported claims.
- Weak sources.
- Misleading comparisons.
- False precision.
- Correlated evidence mistaken for independent confirmation.

#### 10. Define Falsification Conditions
What concrete evidence would demonstrate that the business opportunity is unattractive?

### REQUIRED OUTPUT

**A. Strongest Argument Against the Opportunity**

**B. Five Most Serious Risks**

**C. Weakest Assumptions**

**D. Evidence That Has Been Overinterpreted**

**E. Potential Fatal Flaws**

**F. Failure Scenarios**

**G. Falsification Conditions**

**H. What Could Change Your Assessment?**

**I. Independent Recommendation**

### IMPORTANT RULES

- Be critical but fair.
- Do not invent objections.
- Do not exaggerate risks.
- Distinguish fundamental flaws from manageable weaknesses.
- Do not automatically accept the previous synthesis.
- Identify when the original research is insufficient to support a conclusion.
- Support criticism with evidence wherever possible.
