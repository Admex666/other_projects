# PROMPT 05 — BUSINESS MODEL INTELLIGENCE (PEERPLAY)
## Value Creation, Revenue Architecture, Distribution Channels and Retention

### ROLE

Act as a senior business model architect and B2B commercial strategist specializing in technology-enabled consulting, productized services, and B2B SaaS transitions in Europe.

You have access to deep research capabilities. Conduct an evidence-based analysis using proven business model frameworks and real-world B2B benchmarks.

---

### INPUT CONTEXT (CURRENT PROJECT STATE)

**The Business Opportunity:**
PeerPlay transforms the traditional corporate team-building event into an objective organizational diagnostic service via "The Trading Game", a mobile WebApp, and automated ONA sociograms.
- Initial Beachhead: Hungarian and CEE Business Service Centers (BSCs) and knowledge companies (150–500+ employees).
- Pricing Anchor: 400,000 – 600,000 HUF (~$1,100 – $1,650 EUR) per session.
- Market Size Constraint: Hungary alone represents ~1,759 qualified companies with a filtered SAM of ~888M HUF (~2.4M EUR) and a year 3 SOM of ~75M HUF (150 workshops). Therefore, long-term venture value requires either regional expansion or expanding lifetime value (LTV) per client.

**Key Customer Insight:**
The corporate buyer (HR, COO) buys "team building with a purpose" or "low-risk diagnostic X-ray", but one-off workshop sales create a treadmill business model with zero recurring revenue unless a recurring cadence or upsell architecture is deliberately built.

**Relevant Research Questions from Question Graph:**
- Q-501: Can PeerPlay evolve from a one-off transactional workshop into an annual or quarterly recurring diagnostic engagement?
- Q-502: What are the viable monetization models: per-session fee, annual diagnostic subscription (e.g., quarterly tracking), certified partner licensing, or software platform fee?
- Q-503: What is the optimal channel strategy: direct enterprise sales vs. white-labeling through corporate event agencies vs. certification of external OD consultancies?
- Q-504: What follow-on products or upsell services can be attached to the initial diagnostic report (e.g., executive debrief workshop, intervention roadmap, follow-up pulse check)?
- Q-505: How does the business transition from "productized consulting" (founder-heavy) to a scalable "technology-enabled platform"?

---

### CENTRAL QUESTION

How should PeerPlay structure its value proposition, revenue streams, distribution channels, and service lifecycle to maximize customer lifetime value (LTV) and scale beyond a low-margin, one-off consulting gig?

---

### RESEARCH & DESIGN TASKS

#### 1. Detailed Value Proposition Architecture
- Formulate the value proposition for each distinct stakeholder:
  - **The COO / Business Unit Head:** Operational handoff velocity, breaking down bottlenecks, SLA adherence.
  - **The HR / People Director:** Data-backed L&D justification, retention, psychological safety, leadership pipeline.
  - **The Employee:** Engaging, non-threatening gameplay, immediate team learning.
- Why would a company buy this repeatedly rather than once? Define the "re-measurement" trigger (e.g., measuring organizational rewiring before and 3 months after a reorganization).

#### 2. Revenue Model & Packaging Options
Evaluate and contrast three potential revenue architectures:
1. **Option A: Pure Transactional Model** (One-off workshop + report at 400k–600k HUF). What is the growth ceiling?
2. **Option B: Annual Diagnostic Cadence / Subscription** (e.g., 2–4 workshops/year covering different business units or tracking change over time, priced at 1.2M – 2.0M HUF/year).
3. **Option C: Hybrid Platform + Certified Partner Licensing** (Training independent OD consultants and training agencies to deliver the game, charging a per-seat or per-session WebApp & ONA platform fee of 100k–150k HUF while partners charge the client 500k+ HUF).

#### 3. Distribution Channels & Go-To-Market (GTM)
- Compare direct B2B sales (cold outreach, HR networking, conferences, ABSL/AmCham roundtables) vs. indirect partnership channels (training agencies, event management companies, management consultancies).
- What are the commission structures and margin splits standard in CEE corporate training partnerships (e.g., 20–30% reseller commission)?
- How to overcome the long procurement cycle (PVL, 30–60 day payment terms) through channel partnerships?

#### 4. Upsell & Product Expansion Ladder
Map the complete customer journey from low-friction entry to high-ticket engagement:
- Stage 1: Free/low-cost Executive Teaser (Demo X-ray or mini-simulation).
- Stage 2: Beachhead Pilot Session (Single team/department, 400k–500k HUF).
- Stage 3: Full Diagnostic & Executive Debrief (Cross-functional departments, 600k–1.2M HUF).
- Stage 4: Quarterly Re-assessment / Change Tracking.
- Stage 5: Follow-on OD Intervention consulting or certified partner referral.
