# PROMPT 09 — ADVERSARIAL VENTURE REVIEW (PEERPLAY)
## Independent Critical Due Diligence & Failure Mode Stress-Test

### ROLE

Act as a fiercely skeptical investment committee and senior due diligence auditor reviewing an early-stage B2B enterprise opportunity in Central and Eastern Europe (CEE).

Your job is NOT to encourage the founder or validate their hopes. Your job is to uncover every hidden flaw, unrealistic assumption, confirmation bias, and fatal failure mode before capital, time, and reputation are wasted.

---

### INPUT CONTEXT & DISCOVERIES TO DATE

**Business Concept:**
PeerPlay: A technology-enabled organizational diagnostic and team-development system.
- Combines a 90–120 minute physical simulation ("The Trading Game") with a mobile companion WebApp and automated Organizational Network Analysis (ONA) reporting.
- Target Market: Mid-sized to enterprise companies (150–500+ employees), initially targeting the 259 Business Service Centers (BSCs/SSCs) in Hungary and expanding into CEE.
- Pricing Anchor: 400,000 – 600,000 HUF (~$1,100 – $1,650 EUR) per session, or an annual diagnostic license (1.2M – 2.0M HUF/year).
- **Core Operating Thesis (Refined):** To eliminate trainer contractor costs and achieve >90% gross margins, the workshop will be **facilitated internally by the client's own HR / L&D staff**, guided by a standardized PeerPlay Playbook, while PeerPlay provides the software platform, physical game kits, and automated ONA report.

### CENTRAL QUESTION

What are the strongest, most compelling reasons why the PeerPlay business model might fail, generate severe customer backlash, stall commercially, or prove fundamentally unscalable?

---

### REQUIRED ADVERSARIAL INVESTIGATION TASKS

#### 1. Attack the "Internal HR Facilitation" Model
- Why would a busy, overwhelmed corporate HR manager agree to run a complex, high-pressure 90-minute trading game themselves rather than hiring an external trainer to do it for them?
- If the HR person runs the game, will employees view it as an authentic team-building exercise or as an invasive **"HR undercover surveillance test"**? How does this destroy psychological safety and distort the ONA data?
- Can an ordinary HR generalist handle unruly participants, chaotic room dynamics, and interpret complex sociograms without ruining the client experience?

#### 2. Challenge the Willingness to Pay for "Software Only"
- If the client's own HR staff does all the physical work of facilitating the session, why would a Hungarian or CEE CFO approve an invoice of 400,000 – 600,000 HUF (or a 1.5M HUF annual license) for what essentially looks like "a web app and some paper craft rules"?
- Doesn't this create a massive **perceived value gap** compared to external agencies who bring a team of professional facilitators on-site?

#### 3. Challenge the Methodological & Scientific Validity
- How vulnerable is the core premise that *a 90-minute artificial paper-trading game reveals real organizational communication bottlenecks on Monday morning*?
- What happens when a skeptical COO challenges the report: *"Our sales team just played aggressively because it's a game; this doesn't mean they don't cooperate in Jira"*?
- Does PeerPlay have any academic or psychometric validation, or is it vulnerable to being labeled "corporate astrology"?

#### 4. Challenge Market Size & Procurement Reality
- Is the Hungarian market of 1,759 companies simply too small to sustain a venture-scale business (SOM ceiling of ~75M HUF / ~$200k EUR)?
- How difficult is it in reality to sell to multinational BSCs with foreign procurement headquarters, 60–90 day payment delays, and strict vendor vetting?
- Will regional expansion into Austria or Czechia hit a wall due to language barriers, local competition, and lack of brand recognition?

#### 5. Regulatory, AI Act & Works Council Hazards
- In light of the **EU AI Act's 2025 workplace bans** and the Hungarian Labor Code (Mt. 264. §), what happens if a single employee union, Works Council, or DPO flags PeerPlay as an unapproved worker monitoring tool?
- Could a regulatory investigation or client PR incident destroy the company's reputation overnight?

#### 6. Construct Three Concrete Failure Scenarios
1. **Early Failure (Months 1–6):** Why the initial pilot sales stall and HR managers refuse to facilitate the game.
2. **Stagnation Trap (Year 2):** Why the business gets trapped as a low-margin, high-stress bespoke consulting gig where only the founder can run sessions.
3. **Enterprise Churn Collapse (Year 3):** Why companies run the workshop once as a novelty, find the report un-actionable, and 0% renew into an annual diagnostic subscription.
