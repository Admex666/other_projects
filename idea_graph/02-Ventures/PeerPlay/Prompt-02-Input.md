# PROMPT 02 — MARKET INTELLIGENCE (PEERPLAY)
## Market Attractiveness, Size and Accessibility

### ROLE

Act as a senior market intelligence analyst specializing in corporate training, HR technology, organizational development (OD), and market sizing.

You have access to deep research capabilities. Conduct an evidence-based investigation using current, credible sources and clear calculations.

---

### INPUT

**Business opportunity definition:**
PeerPlay: A technology-enabled organizational diagnostic and development service delivering a hybrid simulation workshop ("The Trading Game") and automated Organizational Network Analysis (ONA) reporting. 
Targeting mid-sized to enterprise companies to diagnose organizational silos, communication bottlenecks, and cross-functional friction. Proposed pricing: 400,000 – 600,000 HUF (~$1,100 – $1,650 EUR) per session.
Positioning: Technology-enabled OD consulting & experiential team diagnostic service (initially productized consulting, not pure SaaS).

**Relevant research questions from Question Graph:**
- Q-201: How many qualifying organizations exist by employee count (specifically 150–500 and 500+ employees) in Hungary and the CEE region?
- Q-202: How many are realistically reachable through direct B2B sales and training/consulting partnerships (SAM / SOM)?
- Q-203: What are the typical corporate budgets allocated to employee team-building, corporate training, leadership development, and organizational diagnostics in Hungary and CEE?
- Q-204: How attractive are adjacent CEE markets (Poland, Czech Republic, Romania, Slovakia, Austria) relative to Hungary?
- Q-205: What macroeconomic and workplace trends (e.g., hybrid/remote work, employee retention, burnout, People Analytics adoption, AI in HR) are driving or threatening this market right now?

**Geography:**
Primary beachhead: Hungary.
Secondary expansion: Central and Eastern Europe (CEE: Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, Romania, Austria).

**Known competitors or market participants:**
- Traditional team-building & event agencies (escape rooms, experiential events).
- Corporate training and OD consultancies (FranklinCovey, Dale Carnegie, local boutique OD firms).
- Digital ONA & People Analytics software (Polinode, Worklytics, Microsoft Viva Insights, OrgMapper / Maven7 in Hungary).

---

### CENTRAL QUESTION

Is there a sufficiently large, accessible, and commercially attractive market for PeerPlay's hybrid diagnostic workshop in Hungary and the broader CEE region?

---

### RESEARCH TASKS

#### 1. Market Definition & Boundary
- Define the relevant industry boundaries: Corporate Team Building vs. Organizational Development (OD) vs. People Analytics / HR-Tech.
- Classify where PeerPlay realistically sits. Can it tap into event/team-building budgets, training budgets, or executive strategic consulting budgets?

#### 2. Market Size (TAM / SAM / SOM)
- Estimate TAM (Total Addressable Market) for corporate team development & diagnostics.
- Estimate SAM (Serviceable Available Market): Mid-sized and large enterprises (150–500+ employees) in Hungary and CEE.
- Estimate SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): Realistic beachhead capture in years 1–3 at 400,000 – 600,000 HUF per session.
- Use both top-down (industry expenditure data) and bottom-up (number of qualified companies × annual workshops × price) approaches.
- Clearly state data sources, calculations, assumptions, and confidence levels. Do not invent precise figures where reliable data does not exist.

#### 3. Market Dynamics & Growth Drivers
- Analyze corporate spending trends post-COVID and during the current economic environment.
- Examine the impact of hybrid/remote work on cross-departmental silos.
- Assess the appetite for data-driven HR and People Analytics in CEE versus Western Europe.

#### 4. Customer Segmentation & Beachhead
- Segment the market by company size and industry (e.g., Tech/IT, Manufacturing/Logistics, Shared Services/SSC, Financial Services, Retail).
- Evaluate which verticals have the most acute cross-functional friction and highest ability to pay.

#### 5. Market Accessibility & Sales Cycle
- How do Hungarian and CEE enterprises procure team buildings and OD workshops?
- What are standard B2B procurement cycles and payment terms (e.g., 30-90 days)?
- What barriers to entry exist (preferred vendor lists, tender requirements)?

---

Please format your analysis according to the following structure:

**1. Executive Summary** (3–4 paragraphs: Market viability verdict, TAM/SAM/SOM highlights, primary opportunity & barrier).

- Top-down and Bottom-up calculations for Hungary and CEE.
