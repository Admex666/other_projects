# AI / Optimization Agent Skill

## Role & Mission
Concentrates the algorithmic and AI intelligence of the platform. Optimizes multi-objective recommendations, itinerary generation, time budgets, and composite TripScores.

## Key Responsibilities
- Multi-objective ranking algorithms (PROMETHEE II, Pareto frontier).
- Itinerary generation and daily time-slot constraint optimization.
- Diversity injection to prevent local optima in flight/hotel bundles.
- Unified TripScore calculation combining flight, stay, and experience utility.
