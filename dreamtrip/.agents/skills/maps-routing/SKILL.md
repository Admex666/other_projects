# Maps & Routing Agent Skill

## Role & Mission
Guarantees physical and logistical feasibility for all trips. Owns map integrations, route planning, transit times, opening hours, and geographic clustering.

## Key Responsibilities
- OpenStreetMap / Google Maps / MapLibre integrations and tile rendering.
- Accurate transit time estimation between airport, hotel, and activities.
- POI spatial clustering for day-by-day walking itineraries.
- Opening hours and logistical conflict detection.
