Round Sequence Placing a card
Over 12 rounds, you will draft cards and place them in your 4-column by 3-row grid.  
When you place a card in your tableau, it first activates itself and then activates all 
orthogonally adjacent cards. 
Activating cards triggers abilities and/or scores you

1. Choose 2 cards 
Secretly select 2 cards from your hand, 
one for your tableau and one for your 
reserve. Keep them hidden from the other 
players, who will do the same.
2. Reveal, Place and Score 
Reveal and place the first chosen card 
face up in your tableau (see Placing a card, 
on the right) and keep the second face 
down in your  reserve. Count the 
  you 
gained, and take the corresponding tokens 
from the shared supply, adding them to 
your reserve.  
Everyone does this simultaneously.
3. Bee time 
The player who gained the fewest 
  
this round, takes 1 
  token from the 
shared supply and adds it to their personal 
reserve. If more players are tied last, each 
of them takes 1 
 .
4. Pass the cards 
Pass your remaining cards to the player on 
your left, then add the card you previously 
placed in your reserve (2) to the new hand 
you received from the player on your right.
5. Deal new cards when needed 
After placing 4 cards (i.e. at the end of 
the 4th and 8th rounds), you will have no 
cards left in your hand.  
At the end of that round, decide whether to 
keep in hand or discard the card currently 
in your reserve.  
Once everyone has made their decision, 
the dealer cuts the deck and deals cards 
until everyone has 5 cards in hand again.
Placement rules
1. Each card after the first must be placed 
orthogonally adjacent to at least one 
other card.
2. At the end of the game, after the 12th 
round, your tableau must be a grid of 
4x3 cards and you may never add a 5th 
column or a 4th row.
Activations
When placing a card in your tableau, you 
first activate that card, then activate each 
orthogonally adjacent card in the order of 
your choice.
